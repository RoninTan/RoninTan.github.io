现在Now

这段时间，我主要在研究高铁桥梁里被地震损伤的桥墩：用 CFRP（碳纤维复合材料）修复之后，抗震性能能恢复多少，对桥梁和轨道又意味着什么。

做法是做试验、做非线性有限元分析，也用可解释的机器学习模型来预测修复效果。导师是江力强老师和郭风琪老师。

These days I spend most of my time on earthquake-damaged bridge piers in high-speed railways. I want to know how much seismic performance a CFRP (carbon-fibre) repair can bring back, and what that means for the bridge and the track.

I work on it with experiments and nonlinear finite-element analysis, and I build interpretable machine-learning models to predict how well a repair will work. My advisors are Liqiang Jiang and Fengqi Guo.
